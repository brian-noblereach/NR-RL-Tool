    How to use this tool

    Assess a research project or early-stage venture across eight readiness dimensions, plus Regulatory for Life Science ventures. Each dimension is scored on its own; there is no overall score.

    • Choose a venture, then edit one of your saved assessments or start a new one. Not listed? Add it from the same list.
    • Score each category at the highest level fully reached. Levels are cumulative. Turn on Life Science venture for health products, and Dual-use venture when the technology is going to both commercial and government buyers.
    • Review & save writes the assessment to the NobleReach database; Save PDF keeps a local copy.
    • Set up the venture name, your name, and whether this is its first assessment.
    • Score each category at the highest level fully reached. Levels are cumulative. A dual-use venture gets separate Commercial and Government scores for Market, Product and Go-to-Market.
    • Download a PDF. First assessments can add notes on the conversation with the team.

    Use your judgment where a venture doesn't match a definition exactly. Deliverables and indicators are examples of evidence, not requirements.
